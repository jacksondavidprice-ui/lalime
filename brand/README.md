# Brand assets

`logo-master-1024.png` is derived from Jackson's full-res master for regen.
Web/Electron icons live in `public/` and `build/` — generated by `scripts/generate-brand-assets.py`.
Do not put the 8MB master under `src/` or `public/`.

## Look and feel

Lalime apps share one look, so people recognize them. This app follows Lalime PC Repair (LAPCRV6).
Tokens live in `src/styles/index.css` under `@theme`; use them rather than raw hex.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `paper` | `#111113` | App background |
| `paper-2` | `#19191c` | Cards, panels, dialogs |
| `surface` | `#232327` | Raised controls, hover |
| `edge` / `edge-strong` | `#333338` / `#44444a` | Borders, dividers |
| `ink` | `#eeeeee` | Primary text |
| `muted` | `#99999e` | Secondary text |
| `lime` | `#2bc500` | Lalime green: primary actions, focus, progress |

Rules of thumb:

- **Gray and Lalime green only.** Green is the single accent; keep it for things that matter
  (the primary button, focus rings, live status). No gradients between colors, no purple.
- **The backdrop is part of the brand.** `bg-atmosphere` + `bg-grain` give the charcoal base,
  a faint lime glow from below, an always-visible 54px grid with a few lit cells near the
  bottom, and a very light, highlight-only film grain. Keep the grain visible but subtle.
- **Type:** Montserrat (display, extra-bold, tight tracking), Figtree (body),
  JetBrains Mono (small uppercase labels and eyebrows).
